Ukraine Barometer · Issue No. 1 · 10–16 November 2026

This week's key points

Resilience index
2.4 / 5
  • The power grid is in its worst state of the season

    More than 310 attacks on energy facilities since the start of November. Scheduled outages in nearly every oblast, and all-day blackouts in parts of the country. Patriot interceptors arrive at about 12 a month, far too few to cover substations.

  • The economy has slipped from slow growth into contraction

    Q3 GDP fell 0.7% year on year, inflation is rising and the NBU raised its key rate to 16.5%. The main drags are labour shortages and power outages.

  • 2027 is short $32.6bn of external financing

    The European Commission tables a €45bn loan on 18 November. Its fate depends on confidence in anti-corruption policy, which the Carthage case is now testing.

  • Talks resumed after a nine-month pause

    Fourth trilateral round in Abu Dhabi. No breakthrough; Donbas is the core dispute. The achievable result is a prisoner exchange of 1,000–1,500 on each side.

  • Strikes on Russian refineries work, but only partly

    Refining capacity is down about 30%, yet crude exports rose to 3.9m barrels a day and October revenue was 65% higher than a year earlier.
